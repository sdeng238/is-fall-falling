# is-fall-falling?
NYC weather is unpredictable, from sunny one day to rainy or even snowy the next, and sometimes a peculiar combination of all of the above. I wanted to capture the idiosyncrasies of what it's like to live in NYC under these conditions, which is a perfect intersection of the two themes of this module--randomness and transitions!
